About the coach

From the ring to your corner

Combat sports shaped me. I became Moroccan National Kumite Champion in 2011, earned my 1st Dan black belt in karate, then competed in boxing with Mouloudia Marrakech Boxing Club and in national kickboxing and Muay Thai finals. The discipline I learned on the mat and in the ring is what I now pass on to my clients.

For 8+ years I've coached everyone from international guests at Fairmont Marrakech to student-athletes at UM6P and members as Head Coach at Alpha Forme Fitness Club. Now I bring that experience to Abu Dhabi: fighter-style training that is smart, intense and always adapted to you, in English, Arabic or French.
